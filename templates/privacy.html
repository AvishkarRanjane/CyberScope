Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

1. Introduction

CyberScope ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Name and email address (when you create an account)
  • Contact information (phone number, address if provided)
  • Payment information (processed securely through third-party payment processors)

Usage Data

  • IP addresses of users accessing our service
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referrer pages

Important Note About Queries

CyberScope does NOT store the content of your searches or queries. When you perform IP lookups, phone number searches, or username scans, we process the request in real-time but do not maintain permanent records of what you searched for.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our services
  • To improve and optimize our platform
  • To communicate with you about updates or support issues
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To prevent fraud and misuse of our services

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for sensitive data at rest
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Secure password hashing (bcrypt)
  • Two-factor authentication options

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • IP API (ip-api.com): For IP geolocation data
  • Phone API (apilayer): For phone number validation
  • Analytics: To understand platform usage
  • Payment Processing: For secure transaction handling

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

6. Your Rights

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Opt-out of certain data processing

7. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. You can control cookie settings in your browser preferences. Note that disabling cookies may affect platform functionality.
